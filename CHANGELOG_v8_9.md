# SteadyShot v8.9 – Clear Red Thermal Signatures

## Changes
- Reworked sandstorm thermal vision to render the complete visible enemy silhouette in saturated, bright red.
- Increased red emission substantially and added a gentle brightness pulse without fading the target.
- Slightly enlarged thermal meshes to make enemy outlines easier to recognize at a glance.
- Darkened the scoped thermal background further to separate bright enemy heat signatures from the storm.
- Preserved the commander briefing/audio and reload HUD improvements.

## Validation
- Source-level checks and ZIP integrity check passed.
- Godot runtime validation was not available in this environment; test thermal mode during an active sandstorm wave before release.
