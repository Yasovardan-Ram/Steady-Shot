# SteadyShot v8.8 – Stronger Thermal Vision

## Changes
- Increased sandstorm thermal enemy visibility: torso and head heat silhouettes are now near-opaque, vivid red, and substantially brighter.
- Increased thermal emission and slightly expanded the heat meshes to make enemy outlines easier to distinguish.
- Darkened and desaturated the scoped thermal view moderately so enemy heat signatures contrast clearly against the sandstorm.
- Preserved the v8.7 reload HUD polish and v8.6 commander briefing/audio.

## Validation
- Source-level checks and ZIP integrity check passed.
- Godot runtime validation was not available in this environment; test a sandstorm wave with thermal sensing enabled before release.
